# Training governance

Verify Phase 0-M selected a trainable role/profile. Curate only eligible local evidence with rights and exact provenance. Pin base/tokenizer/dataset/code/config/GPU. Preserve exact tokens and rollout grouping. Schedule through protected GPU resource manager. Evaluate independently by role/task family and portfolio. Trainer cannot qualify or promote itself.
